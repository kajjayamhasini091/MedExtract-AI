## 1. Executive Summary

In high-acuity emergency and inpatient care, complex patient vignettes and unstructured electronic medical notes create cognitive overload for physicians, resident doctors, and trainees. **MedExtract-AI** is an autonomous clinical intelligence system built natively on **Adaption Labs' Adaptive Data, Blueprint, and Interface infrastructure**.

Given any unstructured clinical scenario (e.g., Emergency Department chest pain presentation, acute diabetic ketoacidosis, or pediatric fever), the system:
1. Deconstructs complex clinical narratives into atomic, categorized diagnostic questions.
2. Synthesizes guideline-concordant, evidence-backed answers (including acute pharmacotherapy loading, contraindicated therapies, and risk stratification).
3. Mitigates clinical hallucinations using Adaption's multi-tier recipe pipeline (Reasoning Traces, Hallucination Mitigation, and Blueprint Steering).
